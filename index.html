Every number comes with its formula and assumptions. Values in these demos are illustrative.

Model: illustrative pump, 3000 rpm and 10 in at full size. Speed and diameter use the affinity laws (flow scales with speed and diameter, head with their squares); trimming is an approximation. System curve: static head plus a flow-squared friction term, steeper as the valve closes. Power = flow × head × SG / (3960 × efficiency) in US units. The operating-region band is a typical guideline, not a limit: use the vendor's allowable range.

Change the speed or the impeller diameter and see flow, head and power follow. Starting values are illustrative.

Q2 = Q1 x (N2/N1) x (D2/D1). H2 = H1 x (N2/N1)2 x (D2/D1)2. P2 = P1 x (N2/N1)3 x (D2/D1)3. Speed and diameter laws are applied one after the other. Efficiency is held unchanged, which is a simplification: trimming typically makes the pump less efficient.

Sources: Pumps.org, trimming impellers (the equations and the Hydraulic Institute 5% statement) and Pumps & Systems, impeller alterations (affinity rule is approximate).

MTBF = operating hours / failures. MTTR = repair hours / failures. Inherent availability = MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR). Definitions vary between organizations (what counts as a failure, whether standby time is included), so state the definition next to every result.

Series: R = R1 × R2 × ... Parallel: R = 1 − (1−R1) × (1−R2) × ... Both assume the parts fail independently over the same period. Values are illustrative.

Score each failure mode from 1 to 10. Agree the meaning of each scale, including the direction of the detection scale, before ranking: results compare only within one analysis.

RPN = Severity × Probability × Detection, as taught in the source course. Names and scores here are illustrative placeholders.

Case: high wheel space temperature after an outage

Sample case drafted from a course case study. Site and company names are removed. It needs the expert's review and a source-permission check before any public release.

What happened

Planned maintenance inspection completedA heavy-duty gas turbine is started afterwards.
Two wheel space channels read above alarmChannels 2AO-2 and 3FO-2 are above their alarm levels.
InvestigationThe as-found thermocouple insertion is checked against the insertion length stamped on the casing.
Finding and fixThe insertion was wrong. A step inside the thermocouple guide tube cap prevented correct insertion. After the cap was machined the thermocouples seated at the correct length.

Background from the course: wheel spaces are cooled by air flowing outward; excessive temperature is alarmed but does not trip the turbine; a bad thermocouple can raise a differential temperature alarm.
